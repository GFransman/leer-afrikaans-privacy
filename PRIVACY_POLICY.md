# Privacy Policy — Leer Afrikaans

**Effective date:** 20 October 2026

Leer Afrikaans ("the app") is an Afrikaans phonics and reading app for children, developed by Garth Fransman ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app handles. The short version: **we do not collect, transmit, or share any personal information.**

## Information we collect

None. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking. We do not operate any server that receives data from the app.

## Information stored on your device

The app stores the following **only on your device**, and it never leaves the device:

- **Learning progress** (for example, which lessons are unlocked or completed) and app settings such as text size.
- **Voice recordings.** If a child uses the "record yourself" feature, the app asks for microphone permission, records a short clip, and saves it in the app's private storage so it can be played back. Recordings are not uploaded or shared. You can remove them at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

## Permissions

- **Microphone:** used only when the child chooses to record their own reading, for playback within the app. You can deny or revoke this permission; the rest of the app will keep working.

## Text-to-speech

The app uses your device's built-in text-to-speech engine to read words aloud. This is provided by your operating system (for example, Google or Apple speech services) and is subject to that provider's own privacy policy. The app does not send text or audio to us.

## Children's privacy

The app is designed for children. Because we collect no personal information, we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13 (or any age). There are no ads, no in-app purchases, no external links in the learning screens, and no social features.

## Third parties

We do not share data with third parties, because we do not have any data. The app does not include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

## Data retention and deletion

All data lives on your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage permanently deletes it.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this location with a new effective date.
